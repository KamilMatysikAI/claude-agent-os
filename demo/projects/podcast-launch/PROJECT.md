# Home-brewing podcast

## Goal

A short weekly podcast for people who brew coffee at home, recorded by Alex, that brings listeners onto the shop's email list. The show is the shop's main free channel until the ad budget proves itself.

## Success criteria

1. Three episodes published and listed in the three main podcast directories by 2026-11-15.
2. 200 email sign-ups tagged "podcast" by 2027-01-31.

## Constraints

- Time: Alex records; about 1 hour a week including the recording itself.
- Budget: hosting at 12 EUR a month (H-002) and one microphone; nothing else without a decision.
- Needs approval: any post or email in the shop's name, any sponsor deal, any guest invitation sent in Alex's name.

## Out of scope

- Video versions of the episodes.
- Paid promotion of the show.
- Sponsor reads before episode 10 (H-012).
