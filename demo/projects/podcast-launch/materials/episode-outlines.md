# Show format and outlines

## Format

- For: people who buy whole beans and brew at home.
- Promise: one thing you can try with your next cup, in 20 minutes.
- Segments: "one bean" (origin and taste), "one mistake" (a common brewing error), "one question" (from a listener, from episode 3).

## Episode 1: Why your coffee tastes flat

- Freshness: roast date versus best-before date
- Grind size, the single biggest variable at home
- One mistake: storing beans in the fridge
- Call to action: sign up for the brewing notes

## Episode 2: Water

- Why the same beans taste different in two cities
- Temperature: kettle off the boil, 30 seconds
- One mistake: reusing water from the kettle

## Episode 3: Pour-over in 4 minutes

- Ratio, bloom, pour in stages
- One question: "Do I need a scale?"

Sources for every factual line are listed under each outline in the working copy.
