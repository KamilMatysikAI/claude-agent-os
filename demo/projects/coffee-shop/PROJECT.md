# Coffee subscription shop

## Goal

Sell a monthly subscription of freshly roasted coffee (two 250 g bags, posted in the week of roasting) from a single landing page, to test whether a small roaster partnership can carry a profitable side business.

## Success criteria

1. Landing page with working checkout live by 2026-10-31.
2. 30 paying subscribers by 2026-12-15.
3. Monthly churn under 10% in the first two billing cycles, measured on 2027-02-15.

## Constraints

- Budget: up to 600 EUR for ads until 2026-12-15, any spend only after an accepted decision.
- Time: Alex has about 3 hours a week for this project.
- People and accounts: one roaster partner (chosen 2026-10-02), shop platform with built-in subscriptions, payment provider account still to be opened.
- Needs approval: any ad spend, any email to the customer list, any public post in the shop's name.

## Out of scope

- One-off coffee sales and gift cards (subscription only until criterion 2 is met).
- Wholesale to cafes and offices.
- Our own roasting equipment.
- A mobile app.
