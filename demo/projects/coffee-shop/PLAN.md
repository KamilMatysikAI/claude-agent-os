## Risks (from /premortem, 2026-09-22)

1. Checkout is not live before the October deadline because the payment account needs business verification. Mitigation: H-005 created early, A-006 prepared in test mode.
2. Ads bring visitors but the offer converts poorly at the chosen price. Mitigation: H-013 feedback round before any spend; H-008 caps the first test.
3. The roaster cannot keep up if orders arrive in a burst. Accepted: volumes under 50 a month are within the partner's stated capacity.
