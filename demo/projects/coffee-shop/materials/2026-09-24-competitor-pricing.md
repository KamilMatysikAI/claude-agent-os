# Competitor pricing: coffee subscriptions (read 2026-09-23)

All shops and prices in this demo are fictional.

**Verdict:** 22 to 26 EUR a month for 500 g with free shipping is the middle of the market. Below 20 EUR leaves no room for ads; above 28 EUR competes with shops that roast to order.

| # | Shop | EUR / month | g / month | Shipping | Pause / skip | EUR per 100 g incl. shipping |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Shop A | 19.00 | 500 | 3.90 | yes | 4.58 |
| 2 | Shop B | 22.00 | 500 | free | yes | 4.40 |
| 3 | Shop C | 24.50 | 500 | free | skip only | 4.90 |
| 4 | Shop D | 26.00 | 500 | free | yes | 5.20 |
| 5 | Shop E | 21.00 | 400 | 2.50 | no | 5.88 |
| 6 | Shop F | 29.00 | 500 | free | yes | 5.80 |
| 7 | Shop G | 32.00 | 600 | free | yes | 5.33 |
| 8 | Shop H | 23.00 | 500 | free | yes | 4.60 |

Excluded: one shop selling only 1 kg bags (not comparable).

## Method

Price per 100 g = (monthly price + shipping) / grams × 100. Computed with a script, not by hand.
