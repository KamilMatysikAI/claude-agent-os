# Payment account sign-up: prepared answers

Copy each answer into the matching field. Nothing secret is stored here; the API keys go into `.env` after the account exists.

| Field | Answer |
|---|---|
| Business type | Limited company |
| Industry | Food and drink, subscriptions |
| Website | the shop's landing page (draft URL until A-004 is live) |
| Product description | Monthly subscription: two 250 g bags of roasted coffee, shipped by post. |
| Average order | 24 EUR |
| Expected monthly volume | under 2,000 EUR in the first 3 months |
| Refund policy | Cancel anytime; unshipped months are not charged. |

You will also need: photo ID and the company registration number.
