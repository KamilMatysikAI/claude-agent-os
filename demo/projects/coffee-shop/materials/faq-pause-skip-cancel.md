# FAQ: pause, skip, cancel (for the landing page)

**Can I pause?** Yes. Pause from your account at any time; nothing is charged or shipped until you resume.

**Can I skip one month?** Yes, up to 3 days before the roast week. After that the month is already roasted for you.

**How do I cancel?** One click in your account. Months not yet shipped are not charged.

**Is there a minimum term?** No, unless you choose the prepaid plan (if it is added after decision H-015).
