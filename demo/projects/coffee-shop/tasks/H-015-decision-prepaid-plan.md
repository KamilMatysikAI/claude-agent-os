# Decision: add a 3-month prepaid plan at 66 EUR

## For approval

Recommend adding a 3-month prepaid plan at 66 EUR (8% off three monthly payments) next to the monthly plan, from launch. Prepaid plans lower churn in the first two cycles (criterion 3) and bring cash forward for the roaster's first order. After acceptance, soft adds the plan in checkout once A-006 is done, and copy adds one line to the landing page. Rejected: 6-month plan (too long a commitment for a new shop).

## Context

Two of the eight competitors in A-001 offer prepaid plans; both at 5 to 10% off.
