# Optional · Selling honestly

Enable this pack if agents write sales material or run anything customers pay for. Numbered S1 to S4.

---

## S1. Do not promise what the product does not do

**Rule.** Sales material (landing pages, emails, PDFs, ads) describes only what exists today. Hard requirements (a separate paid account, a minimum order, a specific device) are stated before the purchase, not in the onboarding email.

**Why.** A customer surprised after paying asks for a refund under the guarantee and stops trusting the seller. For a product that needed a separate paid account, that requirement had to move from the setup guide to the sales page.

**How to apply.** For every claim in a draft, point to the feature, page or document that proves it. No proof, no claim.

## S2. If it cannot be delivered, stop selling

**Rule.** When a product on sale cannot be delivered (file missing, checkout broken, stock out), pause the sale at once and tell the owner. Resuming is the owner's decision.

**Why.** A download link returned "not found" while the product stayed on sale. Every sale in that window turned into a support case.

**How to apply.** Any task that touches a live checkout includes a step "buy it as a customer would" or an equivalent check.

## S3. A brief before any creative or offer

**Rule.** Before generating a creative, an ad or an offer for a business customer, write a 3 to 5 line brief: who will see it and what they should do, what the product is and its main benefit, mandatory elements, and an asset map (which image belongs to which product).

**Why.** The first ad creatives for a new product answered a competitor instead of explaining what the product is. In a batch of two dozen offers, the wrong seasonal box was shown and one product had no image at all; each mistake cost another round of fixes.

**How to apply.** A product named in the text without its own image either gets one or is removed from the text.

## S4. Check licenses before reusing outside material

**Rule.** Any course, template, skill or repository from outside is checked for its license before any part of it goes into something you sell. Permissive licenses with attribution are fine. Paid course content is used only as your own paraphrase of the ideas.

**Why.** Unlicensed third-party material inside a commercial product blocked its sale until it was removed and rewritten.

**How to apply.** Record the source and license in the task log whenever outside material is used.
